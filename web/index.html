Frames: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Earth Information Center. Globe: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation. Sky and Moon: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Deep Star Maps 2020 and CGI Moon Kit. Sun, Moon and planet positions: low-precision formulas from the Astronomical Almanac and JPL (E. M. Standish). The Sun and the Moon are drawn 4.5 times their true size; their positions, the Moon's phase and the planets' positions are computed for the date shown. Sound synthesized live in your browser. Fonts: Cormorant, Michroma, Schibsted Grotesk, Martian Mono (SIL Open Font License). 3D: three.js (MIT).
